Beef and Vegetable Chili

Meal type: Lunch

Servings: 5

Calories per serving: approximately 700

Protein per serving: approximately 48 grams

Ingredients

  • Ground beef — 2 lbs
  • Kidney beans — 2 cans, drained
  • Tomatoes — 2 cans, diced
  • Bell peppers — 2, diced
  • Onions — 2, diced
  • Chili powder — 2 tbsp
  • Olive oil — 2 tbsp
  • Salt and pepper — to taste

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a large pot, add onions and peppers, cook until soft.
  2. Add beef, cook until browned.
  3. Stir in beans, tomatoes, chili powder, salt, and pepper.
  4. Simmer for 30 minutes.

Meal Prep

  • Prepare on Sunday, cool, and portion into containers.
  • Refrigerate up to 5 days.
  • Reheat in the microwave for 2-3 minutes.

Storage Instructions

Store in airtight containers in the refrigerator.

Reheating Instructions

Reheat in the microwave for 2-3 minutes.
