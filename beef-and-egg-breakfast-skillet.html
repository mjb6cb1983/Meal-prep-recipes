Beef and Egg Breakfast Skillet

Meal type: Breakfast

Servings: 5

Calories per serving: approximately 500

Protein per serving: approximately 38 grams

Ingredients

  • Ground beef — 1.5 lbs
  • Eggs — 10
  • Red bell peppers — 2, diced
  • Onions — 2, diced
  • Spinach — 5 cups
  • Cheddar cheese — 1 cup, shredded
  • Olive oil — 2 tbsp
  • Salt and pepper — to taste

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add diced onions and bell peppers, sauté until soft.
  2. Add ground beef, cook until browned. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Stir in spinach until wilted.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk eggs and pour over the beef mixture. Cook until eggs are set.
  5. Sprinkle cheese on top, cover until melted.

Meal Prep

  • Cook on Sunday, cool, and divide into containers.
  • Refrigerate up to 5 days.
  • Reheat in the microwave for 1-2 minutes.

Storage Instructions

Store in airtight containers in the refrigerator.

Reheating Instructions

Reheat in the microwave for 1-2 minutes.
