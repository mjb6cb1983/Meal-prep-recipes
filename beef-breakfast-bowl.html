BEEF BREAKFAST BOWL

Servings: 5

Ingredients

  • Ground beef — 2 lbs
  • Eggs — 10
  • Bell peppers — 3, diced
  • Spinach — 5 cups
  • Olive oil — 2 tbsp
  • Salt and pepper — to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook beef in olive oil until browned.
  2. Add peppers and spinach, cook until soft.
  3. Scramble eggs, combine with beef mixture.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.

Nutrition Information

Calories per serving: approximately 500

Protein per serving: approximately 35 grams
