Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Meal type: Lunch

Servings: 5

Calories per serving: approximately 700

Protein per serving: approximately 47 grams

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast — 2 lbs, sliced
  • Fettuccine pasta — 1 lb
  • Alfredo sauce — 2 cups
  • Parmesan cheese — 1 cup, grated
  • Olive oil — 2 tbsp
  • Salt and pepper — to taste

Directions

  1. Cook pasta according to package instructions.
  2. In a skillet, heat oil and cook chicken until golden brown.
  3. Add Alfredo sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Toss pasta with sauce and chicken, top with Parmesan.

Meal Prep

  • Prepare on Sunday, cool, and portion into containers.
  • Refrigerate up to 5 days.
  • Reheat in the microwave for 2-3 minutes.

Storage Instructions

Store in airtight containers in the refrigerator.

Reheating Instructions

Reheat in the microwave for 2-3 minutes.
