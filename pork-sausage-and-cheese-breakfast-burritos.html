Pork Sausage and Cheese Breakfast Burritos

Meal type: Breakfast

Servings: 5

Calories per serving: approximately 500

Protein per serving: approximately 37 grams

Ingredients

  • Pork sausage — 1 lb
  • Eggs — 10
  • Tortillas — 5 large
  • Cheddar cheese — 1 cup, shredded
  • Salsa — 1 cup
  • Olive oil — 2 tbsp
  • Salt and pepper — to taste

Directions

  1. Cook sausage in a skillet over medium heat until browned.
  2. Whisk eggs, season with salt and pepper, and scramble in olive oil.
  3. Lay out tortillas, fill with sausage, scrambled eggs, cheese, and salsa.
  4. Roll tightly, wrap in foil.

Meal Prep

  • Prepare on Sunday, wrap in foil, and refrigerate.
  • Reheat in the oven or microwave before serving.

Storage Instructions

Store in airtight containers in the refrigerator.

Reheating Instructions

Reheat in the oven or microwave before serving.
