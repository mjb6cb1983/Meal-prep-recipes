Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry

Meal type: Lunch

Servings: 5

Calories per serving: approximately 700

Protein per serving: approximately 50 grams

Ingredients

  • Beef strips — 2 lbs
  • Broccoli florets — 4 cups
  • Soy sauce — 1/2 cup
  • Garlic — 4 cloves, minced
  • Ginger — 2 tbsp, minced
  • Olive oil — 3 tbsp
  • Cooked rice — 5 cups

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a large pan, add garlic and ginger, sauté until fragrant.
  2. Add beef, cook until browned.
  3. Stir in broccoli and soy sauce, cook until broccoli is tender.
  4. Serve over cooked rice.

Meal Prep

  • Prepare on Sunday, cool, and divide into containers.
  • Refrigerate up to 5 days.
  • Reheat in the microwave for 2-3 minutes.

Storage Instructions

Store in airtight containers in the refrigerator.

Reheating Instructions

Reheat in the microwave for 2-3 minutes.
