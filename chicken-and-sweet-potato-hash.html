Chicken and Sweet Potato Hash

Meal type: Breakfast

Servings: 5

Calories per serving: approximately 500

Protein per serving: approximately 36 grams

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast — 1.5 lbs, diced
  • Sweet potatoes — 5, diced
  • Onions — 2, diced
  • Eggs — 5
  • Olive oil — 3 tbsp
  • Paprika — 1 tsp
  • Salt and pepper — to taste

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Toss sweet potatoes with olive oil, salt, pepper, and paprika. Roast for 20 minutes.
  2. In a skillet, cook chicken with onions until browned.
  3. Combine sweet potatoes and chicken mixture.
  4. Fry eggs and place one on each serving.

Meal Prep

  • Prepare on Sunday, cool, and portion into containers.
  • Refrigerate up to 5 days.
  • Reheat in the microwave, add egg just before serving.

Storage Instructions

Store in airtight containers in the refrigerator.

Reheating Instructions

Reheat in the microwave, add egg just before serving.
