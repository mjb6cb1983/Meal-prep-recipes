Pork and Bean Tacos

Meal type: Lunch

Servings: 5

Calories per serving: approximately 700

Protein per serving: approximately 45 grams

Ingredients

  • Pork shoulder — 2 lbs, shredded
  • Black beans — 2 cans, drained
  • Taco seasoning — 2 tbsp
  • Tortillas — 10
  • Cheddar cheese — 1 cup, shredded
  • Salsa — 1 cup

Directions

  1. Cook pork with taco seasoning until tender and shred.
  2. Heat black beans and mix with shredded pork.
  3. Fill tortillas with pork-bean mixture, cheese, and salsa.

Meal Prep

  • Prepare on Sunday, store tortillas separately.
  • Refrigerate up to 5 days.
  • Assemble tacos just before serving, reheat filling in microwave.

Storage Instructions

Store in airtight containers in the refrigerator.

Reheating Instructions

Reheat filling in the microwave before serving.
