High-Protein Beef & Egg Breakfast Burritos

Meal: Breakfast
Servings: 5
Calories: Approximately 500 per serving
Protein: Approximately 40g per serving

Ingredients

  • 1 lb 93% lean ground beef
  • 8 large eggs
  • 1 cup liquid egg whites
  • 5 large whole-wheat tortillas
  • 1 cup reduced-fat shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 cups diced potatoes
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 1/2 cup salsa
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Roast the diced potatoes until browned and tender.
  2. Brown the ground beef with the bell pepper and onion. Season with garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt and pepper.
  3. Scramble the eggs and egg whites.
  4. Divide the beef mixture, potatoes, eggs and cheese evenly among the five tortillas.
  5. Add salsa or keep it separate for serving.
  6. Roll each tortilla tightly into a burrito.

Meal Prep & Storage

Refrigerate portions for the first three days. Freeze later portions after cooling completely.

Thaw frozen burritos overnight in the refrigerator. Microwave until heated completely through before serving.
